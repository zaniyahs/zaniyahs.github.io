About Me

I am a Computer Science student at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa pursuing a B.S. degree, expected Spring 2027. My academic focus lies at the intersection of cybersecurity and software engineering — I am fascinated by how systems can be both elegantly built and rigorously defended.

My path to tech has been shaped by discipline and service. As a member of the Naval ROTC program at UH Mānoa, I helped establish traditions that didn't yet exist — including co-organizing the battalion's first-ever Navy Ball. That same "build from scratch" energy is what I bring to every software project I take on.

Outside the classroom, I am drawn to the ocean, science, and the challenge of solving problems that seem impossible at first. I believe the best engineers are relentlessly curious — and I try to live that out every day.

ICS 314 Final Project

Rainbow Locator

A centralized lost and found web platform for the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa campus, designed to help students, faculty, and staff report missing belongings, browse verified listings, and reconnect items with their rightful owners.

My Contributions
My heaviest contribution was on the design side. I led the product design decisions around how claims and item statuses would be tracked and displayed, shaping the overall look and feel of the platform. I also contributed to UI components using React and Bootstrap 5, ensuring the interface was clean, intuitive, and consistent across the app.
What I Learned
Working in a team using Agile methods and GitHub project boards taught me how to collaborate effectively on a real codebase. I deepened my skills in Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma ORM, and test driven development and gained hands on experience shipping a production-ready application.
